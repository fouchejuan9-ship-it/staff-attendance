# Privacy Policy for Mika's Mind Quest

_Last updated: 29 September 2026_

Mika's Mind Quest is a learning game for children in Grade 2 (ages 7 to 8).

## What we collect

Nothing. The app does not collect, store or share any personal information.

- No account or sign-in.
- No name, email address, location or photos.
- No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
- No analytics or tracking.
- No chat, messaging or links to other websites inside the game.

## What stays on the device

The stars a child earns and the sound on/off setting are saved only on the device the game is played on. They are never sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them.

## Internet access

The game works fully offline and does not need an internet connection.

## Purchases

The free version has no purchases. If a paid "full version" is added later, it will be sold through Google Play or the Apple App Store, which handle payment. The app itself never sees card details. Any purchase button sits behind a grown-ups-only gate.

## Children's privacy

Because the app collects no data at all, it complies with COPPA (USA), GDPR-K (EU) and POPIA (South Africa) requirements for children's apps.
